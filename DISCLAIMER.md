## What Velour does and doesn't do

- Velour is a desktop **player** for your own YouTube Music account. It shows the same pages
  the YouTube Music website shows you and plays music through libmpv.
- It talks to YouTube Music the way the official web app does, using the sign-in of a browser
  you already use. Your cookies are read locally and are only ever sent to YouTube; Velour has
  no servers, accounts, analytics or telemetry.
- It does **not** host, mirror, distribute or include any music, video or other copyrighted
  content, and it does not include or redistribute yt-dlp, deno or any YouTube code. Stream
  addresses are looked up on your machine by yt-dlp, which you install yourself.
- It does not remove ads for free accounts, bypass paid features, region locks or any DRM.
  Premium-quality audio is only available to accounts that have YouTube Music Premium.
- **Offline downloads are turned off by default.** If you turn them on, use them only for
  content you have the right to keep, for personal use, in line with YouTube's Terms of
  Service and the law where you live.
